Student Leadership Academy

Whatever you study, learn to lead.

Your degree builds expertise. The Student Leadership Academy adds another layer: confidence, self-awareness, collaboration, mentorship and the practice of turning ideas into action. Start where you are and build toward bigger impact.

Three progressive tiers

Leadership that grows with you

You do not need to arrive at Dal already thinking of yourself as a leader. The Academy is structured so students can begin with foundations and move toward increasingly ambitious real-world leadership experiences.

01 · FOUNDATIONS

Discover your leadership potential

Build confidence, self-awareness and communication skills. Explore your values, strengths and the kind of leader you want to become.

02 · IN PRACTICE

Put leadership into action

Develop collaborative problem-solving, work with others on real initiatives, and learn how leadership changes when stakes, perspectives and complexity increase.

03 · HIGH PERFORMANCE

From vision to impact

Join an advanced cohort, learn with senior university and community leaders, and lead a capstone project focused on a real-world challenge.

Across every program
