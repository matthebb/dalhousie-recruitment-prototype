Halifax, Nova Scotia

A university city with an ocean attached.

Big enough for music, food, culture, internships and a real downtown. Small enough that campus, cafés, neighbourhoods, parks and the waterfront can still feel connected. Halifax is part of the Dal experience, not just the place around it.

Student city

The right amount of city.

Halifax combines a walkable university core with a working downtown, a major waterfront, beaches, live music, restaurants, professional sport, festivals and a surprisingly deep cultural scene.

The harbour is part of daily life

Walk the boardwalk, take the ferry with your U-Pass, study by the water, or head toward beaches and coastal trails when campus starts to feel suspiciously like campus.

Food, music and late-night Halifax

Downtown, Spring Garden, Agricola and Gottingen put cafés, bars, live music, restaurants, shops and student hangouts within a relatively compact city.

Urban, but never far from green space

Point Pleasant Park, the Public Gardens, Commons, harbour trails and the wider Nova Scotia coast make it easy to leave pavement behind without mounting an expedition.

Find your Halifax

Different neighbourhoods, different versions of student life.

South EndDal’s home base. Campus, historic streets, Point Pleasant Park and lots of student housing.
Downtown & WaterfrontRestaurants, nightlife, festivals, the boardwalk, shops, offices and the harbour all packed together.
North EndArtsy, independent and food-forward, with cafés, shops, restaurants and a more local neighbourhood feel.
DartmouthAcross the harbour by ferry. Alderney Landing, cafés, lakes and a different view back toward Halifax.
Closer than it looks

Getting to Halifax from key Canadian markets.

Halifax Stanfield has frequent non-stop service from major Canadian cities. Routes and schedules change by season, but the basic point is reassuring: moving east does not mean disappearing off the transportation grid.

Toronto
~2h 17m
Non-stop from Pearson, with multiple carriers and very frequent service. Billy Bishop also has non-stop service.

Part of the decision

You are choosing a university. You are also choosing where four years of your life happen.
