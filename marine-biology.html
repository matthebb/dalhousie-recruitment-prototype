The premise

What if the ocean became your laboratory?

Marine Biology at Dal moves from foundational biology into the ecology, physiology, diversity and behaviour of marine life. The Atlantic setting makes the subject tangible: coastal fieldwork, research labs, marine facilities, and intensive experiences can all become part of the degree.
